\section{Laser Driver (Koheron DRV200S-A-200) and Butterfly Mount (BTF100)}
\label{appendix:driver}
The DRV200S is an ultra-low-noise constant-current driver whose setpoint is controlled by an externally applied voltage, with an adjustable current limit and compliance voltage. The -A-200 variant was chosen to match the \SI{58}{\milli\ampere} operating current of the diode. It also provides a DC--\SI{6}{\mega\hertz} modulation input, which is unused in this project.

\begin{table}[H]
	\centering
	\begin{tabular}{@{}lr@{}}
		\toprule
		\textbf{Parameter} & \textbf{DRV200S-A-200} \\
		\midrule
		Laser current range            & \SIrange{0}{200}{\milli\ampere} \\
		Current limit (adjustable)     & \SIrange{65}{300}{\milli\ampere} \\
		Compliance voltage             & \SI{15.5}{\volt} (at $V_{SL} = \SI{19}{\volt}$) \\
		Current noise density (\SI{1}{\kilo\hertz}) & \SI{270}{\pico\ampere\per\sqrt\hertz} \\
		RMS current noise (\SI{10}{\hertz}--\SI{1}{\mega\hertz}) & \SI{265}{\nano\ampere} \\
		Temperature coefficient        & \SI{30}{ppm\per\celsius} \\
		Current monitor gain           & \SI{10}{\volt\per\ampere} \\
		Modulation bandwidth (\SI{3}{\decibel}) & \SI{6}{\mega\hertz} \\
		Supply voltages                & internal \SIrange{4.8}{6}{\volt}; laser \SIrange{3}{19}{\volt} \\
		Compatible lasers              & floating diodes \\
		\bottomrule
	\end{tabular}
	\caption{Key specifications of the Koheron DRV200S-A-200 laser diode driver.}
	\label{tab:drv200s-full}
\end{table}

The diode is housed in a Koheron BTF100 mount for 14-pin butterfly packages (\SI{2}{\ampere} per pin maximum, $51 \times 42 \times 32$\,\si{\milli\metre}), which breaks out the laser anode/cathode and their voltage-sense pins, the monitor photodiode, the thermistor and the TEC to a terminal block.

\section{Temperature Controller (Koheron TEC100L-50)}
\label{appendix:tec}
The TEC100L is a compact high-stability temperature controller with a precision analogue front end and adjustable PID gains. The temperature setpoint is set as a thermistor resistance; the diode's \SI{10}{\kilo\ohm} thermistor falls in the middle of its range.

		Compliance voltage at \SI{1}{\ampere} & \SI{\pm 4.5}{\volt} \\

\end{table}


\section{Balanced Photodetector (Koheron PD100B-AC)}
\label{appendix:pd100b}
The PD100B is an amplified balanced detector with two mounted InGaAs photodiodes feeding a differential transimpedance amplifier. The AC-coupled version was used, so the DC photocurrent from the LO is blocked above \SI{160}{\hertz}.
